## What the plan tells us

- A test about every two weeks. Each unit ends with a "thinking practice" day and sample Thinking questions, so Thinking-category marks matter to this teacher.
- Unit 6 builds on Grade 10 trig (SOH CAH TOA, sine and cosine law). Unit 5 builds on Grade 9/10 exponent laws. Weak foundations there will show up immediately.
- Unit 7's sinusoidal transformations use the same \(a, k, d, c\) transformation ideas as the radical-function unit he's already done.
