Unit 4 Test Card: Rational Functions

Grade 11 Functions · MCR3U

1Transformations: factor out k first

\[ y = a\,f\big(k(x-d)\big) + c \qquad\qquad (x,\,y)\ \to\ \left(\frac{x}{k} + d,\ \ ay + c\right) \]
Horizontal stretch factor is \(\dfrac{1}{k}\).  The shift is the opposite sign of what is in the bracket.

Factor \( y = \sqrt{\tfrac12 x + 4} \;=\; \sqrt{\tfrac12\,(x + 8)} \)

Read \(k = \tfrac12\): horizontal stretch by a factor of \(2\);  \(d = -8\): left \(8\).

Rational \( y = \dfrac{1}{2x - 6} = \dfrac{1}{2(x - 3)} \): compression by \(\tfrac12\), right \(3\).

2Rational functions

\[ y = \frac{a}{k(x - d)} + c \]
Vertical asymptote\( x = d \)
Horizontal asymptote\( y = c \)
Domain\( \{x \in \mathbb{R} \mid x \ne d\} \)
Range\( \{y \in \mathbb{R} \mid y \ne c\} \)
A vertical line is always \(x = \ldots\)   A horizontal line is always \(y = \ldots\)

3Restrictions, holes and asymptotes

First line of every rational question:  denominator \(= 0\ \Rightarrow\ x \ne \ldots\)

\[ f(x) = \frac{x-2}{(x-2)(x+2)} = \frac{1}{x+2},\quad x \ne 2,\,-2 \]
Factor cancelshole at \( \left(2,\ \tfrac14\right) \)
Factor staysvertical asymptote \( x = -2 \)

List restrictions before you cancel. Check factoring by expanding.

4Solving rational equations

\[ \frac{3}{x+3} + 1 = 4 \]

\( x \ne -3 \)  →  \( \dfrac{3}{x+3} = 3 \)  →  \( 3 = 3(x+3) \)  →  \( x = -2 \)

Restriction first. Get the fraction alone. Then multiply by the denominator. Check the answer is not the restriction.

5Inverses

Swap \(x\) and \(y\), then solve for \(y\). Write the answer as \(f^{-1}(x) = \ldots\)

\[ f^{-1}(a) = b \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad f(b) = a \]

For a squared bracket: isolate \((y - h)^2\) first, then take \(\pm\sqrt{\ }\), then choose the sign from the domain.

6Show every step

\[ \sqrt{192} = \sqrt{64 \cdot 3} = \sqrt{64}\,\sqrt{3} = 8\sqrt{3} \] \[ \sqrt{a}\cdot\sqrt{a} = a \qquad 4\sqrt{11}\cdot 6\sqrt{11} = 24 \cdot 11 = 264 \]

An answer with no steps can lose most of its marks, even when it is right.

7Equal signs and final statements

✓ Use \(=\) only between things that are equal.

✗ Never chain different expressions with \(=\). Start a new line instead.

End every question with a statement:

\[ \therefore\ x = -2 \qquad \text{VA: } x = 3,\quad \text{HA: } y = 1 \]
Every step on its own line  ·  equal signs only between equal things  ·  factor out k first  ·  finish with ∴
